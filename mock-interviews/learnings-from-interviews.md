# 📄 Interview Feedbacks

These are insights and lessons learned from my past interviews. They help guide future preparation and mindset.


## 🏢 Atlassian

* **Role Alignment:** When applying for leadership or senior roles (e.g., Principal Engineer), **showcase the work you're already doing at a comparable level** in your current company.
* **First Impression Matters:** Use the **prescreening or first round** to clearly articulate responsibilities, ownership, and impact areas that match the role you're targeting.


## 🏢 GE Vernova

* **System Design (Architect Round):**

  * Interviewers may ask for basic systems (e.g., Franchise Store System). The goal is **not perfection** but **depth in discussion**.
  * **Key Areas to Cover:**

    * Clarify requirements clearly before jumping into design.
    * **Discuss trade-offs** thoughtfully — e.g., *Latency vs. Consistency*, *Cloud vs. Edge vs. On-Premises*.
    * Keep focus on **scalability, availability, security, and maintainability** even for basic systems.


## 🏢 Unisys

* **Coding Round:**

  * You only have 1 hour — aim for a **working solution first**, then optimize.
  * Remember:

    > ❝ A non-optimized solution is better than a non-working solution ❞
* **Problem Solving:**

  * Think aloud — **explain your thought process** and justify decisions as you go.


## 🏢 VISA

* **Self-Awareness & Communication:**

  * Listening is just as important as speaking.
  * Be attentive to cues, questions, and explanations shared by the interviewer.
  * Reflect and adapt during the discussion based on what's being shared.
